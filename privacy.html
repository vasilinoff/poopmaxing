Privacy

PoopMaxing · last updated 14 October 2026 · Deutsch

PoopMaxing doesn't know who you are and doesn't want to.

  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking across apps. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.
  • Your salary, currency, working hours and session history are stored only on your device and are deleted with the app. Your salary amount never leaves the phone.
  • Everything that does go to our server is tied to a random player ID, not to you. “Delete my data from the server” in Settings removes it.

Who is responsible

PoopMaxing is made by Vasily Ponomarev, Amsterdam, the Netherlands, who is the controller for the data described here. Contact: vaspondm@gmail.com.

Anonymous statistics (your choice)

Where the GDPR applies (EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland, judged by your device's region) nothing is collected until you tap “Sure” when the app asks; elsewhere statistics are on until you switch them off. Either way you can change it at any time in Settings → “Anonymous usage stats”; switching off also deletes the installation ID.

When on, the app sends usage events to Google Analytics for Firebase: which screens and features are used (onboarding with pay period, currency and weekly hours but not the amount; sessions with their length; the wardrobe, the map, duels and the ranking; sharing; errors). Firebase adds technical data: device model, iOS and app version, coarse region and a random installation ID. It isn't linked to your identity or used for advertising. Legal basis: your consent (in the EU/EEA, UK and Switzerland), otherwise our legitimate interest in improving the app. Google keeps the events for at most 14 months; see Firebase privacy and Google's Privacy Policy.

Photo check, “Poopy's lab” (optional)

Only if you tap “Send to the lab” and agree: the photo you take of your poop is sent to our server and passed to Claude by Anthropic, which estimates its size and shape. We don't store or log the photo, and it isn't linked to you; Anthropic handles it under its API data policy. Only the result (size, shape and Poopy's comment) is kept on the server for one hour, so you can leave it on the map as a pile. Nothing is sent unless you start it. Legal basis: your consent.

District map and piles (optional)

Only if you allow location. After a session the app sends the number of the ~2 km square it started in and how long you sat, with your random player ID and nickname. Your exact position is not stored. Squares reset every week.

  • Leaving a pile: your phone asks Apple Maps for a public place nearby (a café, a park, a stop) and only that place's position is sent.
  • Collecting a pile: after a session your position is sent once, compared with nearby piles and discarded at once.
  • District and street names (“You're here: …”) are looked up by Apple Maps on your phone.
  • Sabotage and pile pickups are kept as events for up to 14 days so the other player can be told about them.

Nickname and your Poopy

Other players see you by a generated nickname (a word and a number, e.g. “Swirlykins 1-85”), never your name. If you type your own nickname, the text is sent to Claude by Anthropic to check it isn't offensive, then stored on our server and shown on the map, in the ranking and in duels. Anyone can report a nickname; after three reports it's reset to the generated one. The Poopy you wear (its shape and outfits) is sent with your player ID so others see it next to your nickname.

Purchases

Outfits are bought through Apple; we never see your payment details or Apple ID. So that others can see a bought look, the app sends Apple's signed receipt for it to our server, which checks it with Apple's keys. For the numbered Solid gold look the server keeps the transaction ID, the number and the player ID wearing it; the number stays with the purchase after “Delete my data from the server”.

Device check

To keep fake apps off the map and the ranking, the app uses Apple's App Attest: the server keeps a public key made by your iPhone's secure chip for your random player ID and checks that requests come from the real app. It says nothing about you.

Weekly ranking (optional)

Only if you join it: your time on the throne and number of sessions this week, with your player ID, nickname and Poopy, so other players see where you stand. No money, no location. You can leave the ranking at any time.

Duels (optional)

Only if you challenge a friend or accept a challenge: while a duel is on, the app sends the start time and length of your sessions of a minute or more, with your player ID and nickname. No money amounts, no location. The server keeps these sessions for 40 days so finished duels can show their result. The name you give your opponent stays on your phone.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Game Center, your time on the throne (weekly minutes, longest session, number of sessions) goes to Apple's leaderboards under your Game Center nickname. Money amounts are never sent. Apple's privacy policy applies.

Who processes it

  • Cloudflare: our server and database (privacy policy)
  • Google Firebase: statistics, only as described above
  • Anthropic: the lab and nickname checks
  • Apple: place names, purchases, App Attest, Game Center

Some of them process data outside the EU under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, object to its use, and withdraw consent at any time (in the app's Settings). “Delete my data from the server” deletes your districts, piles, duels, ranking entry, nickname, look and device key in one tap. Because we don't know who you are, we can only find your data by the player ID on your phone; write to vaspondm@gmail.com if you need help. You can also complain to a data protection authority, in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.

Конфиденциальность (по-русски, кратко)

Аккаунтов, рекламы и слежки между приложениями нет; зарплата, часы и история заседаний хранятся только на телефоне. Всё, что уходит на сервер, привязано к случайному ID игрока, и кнопка «Удалить мои данные с сервера» стирает это. Отвечает за данные Василий Пономарёв, Амстердам: vaspondm@gmail.com.

Анонимная статистика (Google Analytics для Firebase: какие экраны и функции используются, без суммы зарплаты) в ЕС, Великобритании и Швейцарии включается только после твоего «Конечно», в остальных странах включена; выключается в настройках в любой момент. «Сдать на анализ» (по согласию): фото уходит через наш сервер в Claude от Anthropic и не хранится; результат хранится час, чтобы оставить кучу. Карта (если разрешишь геолокацию): номер квадрата ~2 км и время; точное место не хранится, для кучи отправляется публичное место рядом, а при сборе куч позиция сверяется один раз и сразу удаляется. Ник (сгенерированный или свой — свой проверяет Claude) и надетая Пупа видны другим игрокам. Покупки идут через Apple; сервер проверяет чек Apple, а для именного «Золотого стандарта» хранит номер. App Attest хранит ключ устройства для случайного ID. Рейтинг и дуэли — только если вступишь, без денег и места; дуэли хранятся 40 дней. Обработчики: Cloudflare, Google, Anthropic, Apple. Можно запросить доступ, исправление и удаление данных и пожаловаться в Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.
